Our Mission

Clean clothes aren't a luxury — they're a necessity.

For millions of families, the cost of laundry is a hidden burden. When parents have to choose between food and clean clothes, kids are the ones who suffer. They miss school, lose confidence, and face bullying.

The Laundry Society exists to change that. We partner with schools, community centers, and local dry cleaners to provide free laundry and dry cleaning services for children in need. Every child who walks through our doors leaves with fresh, clean clothes and a little more dignity.

We believe something as simple as a clean shirt can change a child's entire day — and over time, their entire trajectory.

How It Works

A simple process that makes a big difference.

1

Schools Identify Kids

Teachers and counselors discreetly identify students who need help with clean clothing, ensuring no child is singled out or embarrassed.

2

We Collect the Clothes

Our volunteers pick up laundry from schools and families on a regular schedule, making it easy and stigma-free for everyone involved.

3

Professional Cleaning

Partner dry cleaners and laundromats wash, dry, and press every item with care — treating each piece like their own.

4

Fresh Clothes Returned

Clean, folded clothes are returned to the children within 48 hours. Ready to wear, ready to face the world with confidence.
